Privacy

This tool runs entirely in your browser. There is no server, no account, no analytics, and nothing tracking you or anyone else who opens this page. Your tags, characters, presets, history, and settings are saved only on this device, using your browser's own local storage — they are never sent anywhere. The optional Ask AI tab is the only part of this app that ever contacts the internet, and only when you type in your own key and press Ask — sent straight from your browser to the provider you picked, never through this site. Hosting a page like this one may still involve ordinary web-hosting records (such as your IP address) kept by whoever hosts it, under their own policy — that is normal for any website and outside this app's control.

Want a clean slate, or handing this device to someone else? This clears every saved tag, character, preset, and setting from this browser — it cannot see or touch anything on any other device.

Ask AI experimental

Describe an idea, and an AI you connect yourself turns it into tags sorted into the categories above. This needs your own API key from a provider — nothing is included or paid for by this app.

⚠ This may not work. Browsers often block a web page from calling an AI service directly for security reasons (this is called CORS). If you see a network error below, that's what's happening — it's not something this app can fix from here.
